# AI Team for Thesis Research

This folder defines a local AI research team for my thesis project.

The purpose is not to let one AI write the thesis alone, but to let multiple AI agents play different roles:

- Claude Code: architect, theorist, reviewer, and skeptic.
- Codex: formal derivation assistant, symbolic checker, implementation planner, and numerical verifier.
- Human author: final decision-maker.

The AI team must discuss the thesis project as a research project, not merely as a coding project.

The current thesis concerns asymmetric-information contests, endogenous timing, and the role of correlation between informed and uninformed players' valuations.

The central question is:

> Is the informed player's preference for moving second driven by timing itself, or by the signal-belief-response mechanism induced by correlated valuations?

The team should help with:

1. model construction;
2. equilibrium derivation;
3. incentive compatibility checks;
4. comparison among SS, UI, and IU regimes;
5. locating non-empty parameter regions;
6. welfare and dissipation analysis;
7. exposition, notation, and theorem/lemma structuring;
8. checking whether claims are stronger than what the model proves.

The team should not silently overwrite the author's intended research direction.
